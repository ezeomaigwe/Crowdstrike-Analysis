# Crowdstrike-Analysis
Analysis of CrowdStrike glitch in 2024
This case study primarily focuses on the CrowdStrike glitch that occurred during
software update which caused a global IT outage that affected millions of systems running the
Falcon security sensor. This glitch resulted from a failure in CrowdStrike’s internal testing
process and validation. The Outage resulted in losses and lawsuits filed against CrowdStrike
which led to huge payouts. Some industries affected by the outage include government agencies,
healthcare, retails and aviation companies such as Delta. Although the CrowdStrike incident was
not an actual cyberattack, it helps organizations determine the “what-if” potential downstream
business impact of a cyberattack at any supply chain. CrowdStrike strengthened its testing and
deployment policies while businesses firm reevaluate their dependencies on third-party security software.
